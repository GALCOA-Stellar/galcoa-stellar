# Customer discovery: interviews with three Argentine distributors

**What this is.** Early customer-discovery evidence for GALCOA+ on Stellar: three short interviews with distributors in Argentina about how they record deliveries, whether they have been unable to prove a delivery, and what brands ask from them to build trust. The first two interviews took place on 20/10/2026 and the third shortly after. The same three questions were asked to each person.

**How to read it.** The interviewees agreed to be quoted by name and company; contact details are not stored in this repository. Quotes are translated from Spanish (originals in [`ENTREVISTAS-RESUMEN.md`](ENTREVISTAS-RESUMEN.md)). These are conversations with people the team knows, not a statistical sample, and they show declared interest, not contracts or payments.

## Interviewee 1: Rodrigo Olmedo, Cleanshop Rcia (cleaning-supplies distributor, NEA)
Instagram: instagram.com/cleanshop.rcia
- **How do you record deliveries today?** "Today we work with a signed paper delivery note, and the driver tells us on WhatsApp when he finishes unloading."
- **Have you ever had a claim you could not prove?** "Yes, it happened to us. Sometimes the delivery note comes back illegible or gets lost, the customer says goods were missing, and we end up absorbing the cost so we do not lose them."
- **What did brands ask from you to trust you?** "Big brands ask for sales history, the size of our logistics fleet and the number of shops we cover in the area."

## Interviewee 2: Joaquín Deffot, Runea (official distributor for Bodegas Colón wines)
Website: runea.com.ar. He spoke from the brand side: he said he would ask his distributor portfolio for a system like GALCOA+.
- **How do you record deliveries today?** "Dispatch from the winery goes out through a system (ERP), but in the 'last step' we depend on the distributor, who usually still uses paper delivery notes and WhatsApp reports."
- **Have you ever had a claim you could not prove?** "Yes, it is a frequent problem. It happens a lot with incomplete boxes, broken bottles or vintage changes. If the paper arrives stained, without a clear signature or gets lost, we do not know whether the problem was in transport or at the shop, and the brand ends up bearing the replacement cost."
- **What do you ask a new distributor to give them your representation?** "We ask for a verifiable client portfolio, storage conditions (essential for wine) and the technology capacity to guarantee traceability and care of our bottles up to the point of sale."

## Interviewee 3: Francisco, Congela2 (distributor of frozen food, cold cuts, dairy, burgers, bread and ice cream)
He spoke in the first person: he makes the deliveries himself.
- **How do you record deliveries today?** "Physical spreadsheet. The digital part is WhatsApp: he sends the customer their balance by phone."
- **Have you ever had a claim you could not prove?** "Yes, when the customer forgets, or when the employee is incompetent." He says he makes the deliveries himself and had problems.
- **What did brands ask from you to trust you?** "Our customers trust us, so we never had problems in that sense. Some brands ask for a very high minimum amount; there, if you show as a company that you use technology or that you keep your data, you have a better chance of being given goods on consignment or of building a long-term relationship."

## What they show (and what they do not)
- All three describe the same pain: a paper delivery note that gets lost or comes back illegible, and **someone ends up paying the cost** of what cannot be proven (the distributor or the brand).
- Brands ask would-be representatives to **prove history and capacity**. For interviewee 3, showing that you use technology and keep your data helps to get goods on consignment or a long-term relationship.
- One interviewee said he would ask his distributors for the system, and interviewee 3 said he would take part in a pilot with his distributor: this is **declared interest**, not a contract or a payment.
- **They do not mention payment delays or deferred payment terms.** Payment against confirmation remains a hypothesis to test in the sprint.
- Three interviews are not a statistical sample.
